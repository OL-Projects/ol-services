Included in the price

Four deliverables, one round of changes.

  • A three-page intake form, in French and English, built from the questions your clinic chooses.
  • A fillable, printable PDF — no software to install and nothing to host.
  • A patient-facing explainer: why the form exists and how to return it to your clinic.

Clear limits

The clinic receives the answers. OL sees none of them.

OL builds the document; your clinic receives the patient responses and decides what information to collect, how long to keep it and which return channel to use. OL needs no patient data and hosts no form and no medical record. The kit is not clinical or legal advice, and it connects to no practice-management software.

How it runs

Three steps, no subscription.

  1. Write to us with the clinic name and your current questions empty — no patient data. We agree on the scope.
  2. You receive a first version within five business days of receiving your information and logo.
  3. You read the kit and request up to one round of changes. If it is not right for you, nothing is owed; otherwise we issue the invoice.
