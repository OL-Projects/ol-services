What is deliberately not in it

No homeowner or applicant name, no phone, no email — the product is project intelligence from public records, and personal data stays out of the file by design. A territory licence is sold once per city: if a competitor holds it, you are told before you pay.

How it is made

A deterministic pipeline, not a manual watch.

A pipeline reads the registers, extracts the dossiers, ranks the projects by value and recency, then renders this page from a database — no hand-typing, no estimate written by a person. That is also why it can be priced this low: the marginal cost of a copy is compute.

  • The register is the source. Address, type of work and permit reference come from it; OL does not correct them and does not complete them.
  • "Estimate" is not "declared". A value marked estimate is OL's model; the range and its basis are shown with it, never presented as a settled amount.
  • A filed permit is not a guaranteed job. The register says what was applied for: a permit can be amended, delayed or abandoned, and the reference is the only checkable anchor.
  • No personal data. No name, no phone, no email in the file — a design constraint, not a setting.
