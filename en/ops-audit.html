What you get

A document, not a conversation.

  1. 1A written report: the problems, the fragile parts and what works well, ranked by what they cost you if they stay as they are.
  2. 2Every finding tied to a file, a line or a request — no general advice, no filler.

What we can read

Python and test suites

Failing tests, flaky tests, tests that pass without asserting anything.

Data and pipelines

Silent data loss, timezone bugs, money handled as floats, retries without idempotency.

Automations

n8n, Make or Zapier flows that quietly stopped running, or that send when they shouldn't.

Documents

Quotes, invoices and PDFs that break when a name is long or a total is large.

What we refuse

  • Anything requiring your passwords or production credentials — you run it, you paste the output.
  • Health, financial or personal data. No job that needs it.
  • A promise that the audit will make you money. It describes the real state of the system; what you do with it is yours.
  • Findings we cannot support. If the line cannot be shown, it does not go into the report.

The guarantees, the same as everywhere
