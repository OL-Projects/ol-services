Privacy

This site collects nothing.

Short version: no cookies, no third-party scripts, no newsletter. What you send by email is used to answer you, and nothing else.

What this site stores

No cookies. No local storage. No fingerprinting. No third-party scripts, fonts or pixels. The page is one HTML file and one stylesheet served by GitHub Pages; GitHub logs requests as any web server does, under its own policy, and that log is not available to us as individuals.

What you send us

Requests go from your own mail application to support@olcontrol.com. The page itself transmits nothing: the form composes a message on your device and you send it yourself. An email we receive is used to answer your request, to prepare a quote or a document, and for nothing else. It is not added to a mailing list, because there is no mailing list.

How long we keep it

Correspondence is deleted within 24 months of the last exchange, unless it is attached to an invoice that accounting law requires us to keep longer (Québec: 10 years for tax records). Documents produced for you are kept for the same period and then deleted. Plans are not sold, not shared with another client, and not used to train a model.

Your rights

Under Québec's Law 25 you may ask what we hold about you, ask for a copy, ask for a correction, or ask for deletion. Write to the address above; a request is answered within 30 days. The person responsible for the protection of personal information can be reached at that same address.

Automated decisions

None. No profile is built about you, and no decision about you is made by software alone. The estimating work is done by an AI agent, but every deliverable is a document you read, accept or refuse, and that a licensed contractor signs.

Hosting

This site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States). No application server of ours receives your visit.

Date and version

Dated 13 November 2026. If this policy changes, the change is published on this page with a new date, and anything already received stays governed by the version in force when it was received.
