What the document actually contains

  1. Quantities derived, not guessed. Floor, perimeter, gross and net wall area, with the waste factor per trade stated on the page. You can check every number by hand.
  2. MasterFormat divisions — general requirements, demolition, plumbing, electrical, finishes, fixtures — each line with quantity, unit and value.
  3. Subtotal, GST and QST, arithmetically checked before it is sent, and a plain statement of what is excluded.
  4. Watermarked on every page as a free sample with your address on it, so it can never be mistaken for a quote or passed off as one.
Why free? Because you should see the format before you pay for it — and because a sample that shows the arithmetic is the shortest way to explain what a real takeoff buys you. OL holds no RBQ licence and does not perform construction work; the document is an estimating deliverable.
