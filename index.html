It writes

From a plan, a file or a few sentences — an estimate, a takeoff, an audit or a report, in your own format.

It measures

The document is rendered and read back: margins, page count, every line, and the sum of the parts against the printed total.

It rewrites

What fails goes back for another pass. What still fails is not delivered. You are told which line failed and why.

The difference is one line.

A quote that says $3,056.90 next to line items that add to $2,956.00 is not a rounding error. Someone signs it, builds it, and eats the difference.

What it cannot do

A short list, because a company that cannot name its limits has not tested them.

It cannot check a price you never gave it. It verifies structure and arithmetic, not whether your supplier quoted you fairly.
It cannot visit the site. No measurements from a room it has never seen. You supply the plan or the numbers.
It runs on your key. If your provider is down or out of credit, so is OL. Nothing is silently routed through us.
