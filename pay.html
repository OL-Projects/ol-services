How to pay

Two ways. Both produce an invoice with GST and QST shown separately, which is what your accountant wants.

1 · Interac e-Transfer — works today

Send to

spirokaglis@gmail.com

  1. Send the agreed amount by Interac e-Transfer to the address above.
  2. In the message field, put the job reference we agreed (for example AUDIT-001).
  3. No security question is needed — the address is registered for direct deposit.
  4. You receive a numbered invoice with GST 5 % and QST 9.975 % broken out, usually the same day.

Nothing is charged until we have agreed the scope and the price in writing.

2 · Card — being enabled

Card payments via Stripe

The Stripe account is in setup. Once it is live this page will carry a payment button that takes Visa, Mastercard and Amex, and issues the receipt automatically.

If you need to pay by card before then, say so and you will be sent a direct payment link the same day.

If something goes wrong

Write to spirokaglis@gmail.com with the job reference. If a payment was taken for work that was not delivered, it is refunded in full.
