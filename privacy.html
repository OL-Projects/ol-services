Privacy

Short version: this website collects nothing. What you send by email is used to answer you, and nothing else.

What this site stores

No cookies. No local storage. No fingerprinting. No third-party scripts, fonts or pixels. The page is a single HTML file served by GitHub Pages; GitHub logs requests as any web server does, under its own policy, and that log is not available to us as individuals.

What you send us

Requests are made from your own mail application to spirokaglis+ol@gmail.com. Nothing is transmitted by the page itself. An email you send is used to answer your request, to prepare a quote or a document, and for nothing else. It is not added to a mailing list, because there is no mailing list.

How long we keep it

Correspondence is deleted within 24 months of the last exchange, unless it is attached to an invoice that accounting law requires us to keep longer (Québec: 10 years for tax records). Documents we produce for you are kept for the same period and then deleted.

Your rights

Under Québec's Law 25 you may ask what we hold about you, ask for a copy, ask for a correction, or ask for deletion. Write to the address above; a request is answered within 30 days. The person responsible for the protection of personal information is Spiro Kaglis, reachable at that same address.

Automated decisions

None. No profile is built about you, and no decision about you is made by software alone.

Hosting

This site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States). No application server of ours receives your visit.
